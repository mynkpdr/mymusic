About, privacy and terms

What this is

A web music player that finds and plays YouTube videos as audio (with an optional video view). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube, Google, or the authors of any Android app. Names and trademarks belong to their owners.

Content

This site hosts no media. Everything played or downloaded comes from YouTube and belongs to its creators and rights holders. You are responsible for how you use it: stream and download only what you have the right to, and follow YouTube's Terms of Service and your local law.

Downloads

Downloads (M4A, MP3, MP4) may be switched off on this site. Saving YouTube videos can break YouTube's Terms of Service and copyright law, and converting them takes a lot of server power. Streaming is not affected.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no cookies, no tracking or analytics.
  • Your likes, playlists, history, settings and the taste profile used for recommendations are stored only in your browser (local storage). They are never sent to this server. Use Settings → Backup & transfer to move them to another device, or clear your browser data to erase them.
  • The server sees the searches and video ids you request, and like any web server it handles your IP address to answer you. It keeps no accounts and does not store that data for profiling.
  • Thumbnails load directly from YouTube's image servers, so YouTube sees your IP address for those requests. The server also asks the community SponsorBlock service for skip segments using the video id only.

Fair use of the service

Requests are rate limited, downloads are limited in number and length, and the service may be slowed, changed or stopped at any time without notice. It is provided as is, with no warranty.
