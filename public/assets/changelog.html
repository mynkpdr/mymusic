Web edition

  • Search and play any YouTube video as audio, with an optional video view.
  • Queue, playlists, likes, history and a download dialog with format and quality choices.
  • Recommendations that learn from what you play, stored only in your browser, with export and import.
  • Opus audio when your browser supports it, equalizer, playback speed and pitch, sleep timer, SponsorBlock skipping.
  • Open /watch?v=<id> links to play a video straight away.
  • Keyboard shortcuts (press ?).
